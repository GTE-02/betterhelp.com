Our AI mission at BetterHelp

Our mission at BetterHelp is to champion the well-being in all of us and eradicate the barriers to mental wellbeing across the globe. This is reflected in our investment in AI - we believe access to innovative mental healthcare is paramount regardless of someone's gender, age, race, religion, or income.

Our approach to technology is to build responsibly with licensed clinicians to enhance the way you find, experience, and stay connected to your therapist. Our AI development prioritizes clinical and legal oversight, and is measured against the lived experiences of clients and therapists who use our technology each day.

Our responsible AI principles

At BetterHelp, each new AI tool or feature we build undergoes a formal AI Impact Assessment and approval process across Legal, Security, Clinical, Product, and Data/AI leaders to ensure it meets the highest standards of safety, transparency, and clinical integrity before being introduced to clients and therapists. Our responsible AI principles include:

  • Clinical oversight and regulatory compliance

    The AI features we build are reviewed and continuously monitored by our experienced clinical and legal teams to ensure it supports your therapy experience while meeting robust healthcare and privacy standards.

  • People-powered care

    AI still can't replicate empathy. BetterHelp gives you access to real, licensed therapists who provide high quality care. Our AI doesn’t provide therapy, diagnosis, or treatment, and we prohibit therapists from using third party AI tools (like ChatGPT) for therapy communications.

  • Privacy, security and risk management

    Our AI program was developed in alignment with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. This framework ensures we build, deploy, and use AI systems in a safe, responsible, and trustworthy way.

  • Bias mitigation

    All new AI tools are tested and designed to monitor any bias across race, religion, gender, and other identities.

  • Transparency

    You are in control. AI features are clearly disclosed, explained in plain language, so you know where and how it is being used.

Frequently asked questions

More FAQs

How does BetterHelp define “AI”?

When we say “AI,” we mean technology— often based on large language models (LLMs) or other advanced systems— designed to make things simpler and more effective. Examples include tools that organize data, reduce administrative tasks, or generate text. These tools are built to support, not replace, the lived experiences of clients and therapists.

Is AI replacing my therapist?

No. Therapy at BetterHelp is provided by licensed therapists. Our AI tools and features are designed only to support our therapists and clients, not diagnose, treat or make clinical decisions.

What is a “clinical decision”?

A clinical decision is any professional judgment related to your care, such as making a diagnosis, creating a treatment plan, or choosing a therapeutic approach. These decisions are always made by credentialed therapists, not AI. Clinical decisions are grounded in evidence-based practice, professional expertise, patient preferences, and ethical principles. Therapist matching, by contrast, is an administrative tool that simply helps connect you with a therapist and is never involved in clinical care.

How does BetterHelp address bias in its AI tools?

We consistently monitor and audit our AI features for bias so they are designed to provide everyone equitable access to care. Our Responsible AI Committee leaders review AI features to ensure they work fairly across different backgrounds, identities, and needs.

How does AI help therapists?

Our AI therapist tools reduce administrative work and support therapists in focusing on the care they provide you in and between sessions. This means more energy goes into your session, not operational tasks.

Do BetterHelp therapists use third-party AI tools like ChatGPT to write responses during sessions?

BetterHelp therapists are required to conduct all clinical responses themselves. Our policies prohibit therapists from using third party AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) for therapy communications with the goal of ensuring that every message reflects the therapist's expertise, empathy, and clinical judgment, consistent with our Responsible AI policies prohibiting third-party AI tools in therapy communications. For more details on therapist responsibilities and platform rules, see our BetterHelp Terms & Conditions.

Who oversees how BetterHelp uses AI?

BetterHelp's Responsible AI Committee reviews every new AI tool before launch. This group includes leaders across Legal, Clinical, Security, Product and Data/AI who work together to ensure tools are safe, ethical, and clinically appropriate. For more about how BetterHelp operates and answers to common questions, see our BetterHelp FAQ.
