The BetterHelp Culture

Always getting better

Our drive to improve people's lives continues to propel us forward. We are constantly questioning, learning, and adapting. Growth is a core component of our culture and we will not stop evolving to be the best we can be!

Choose your own adventure

You will take ownership of your path here and help write BetterHelp’s next chapter! Our shared curiosity drives every member of the team to learn, grow, and take initiative to contribute to our overall success.

Open up

We commit to transparency by celebrating candid and authentic conversations, efficient meetings, and curated spaces for the team to connect on all levels. As a result, we are educated in efforts across the entire organization.

Better together

We are a human centric company, both in our product and in the community we create in our workplace. We play nice and leave our egos at the door. And honestly, we just have a good time!

Perks of BetterHelp

Collaborative culture

We’re a team. Through a variety of virtual and in-person events, on-sites, and off-sites, we create connections and ensure you’re a part of the team.

Generous compensation

Your financial wellness is a priority of ours. We offer competitive compensation, 401K, ESPP, and more.

Holistic wellness package

Your comfort and wellness are important to us. We provide a generous WFH stipend, access to mental health & wellness tools, health benefits, and more.

Family friendly

Family comes first. Our ultra-flexible PTO and generous parental leave ensure your whole family is welcome and supported.

Remote flexibility

The work world is changing and so are we. Our flexible work from home and office plan provides the benefits of collaboration while embodying flexibility.
