Frequently Asked Questions

What is Drozo Therapy? +

Drozo Therapy is a private, confidential telehealth sanctuary where clients can book sessions with certified practitioners, store encrypted personal documents, and track clinical outcomes.

Can I choose between Discord voice and Jitsi video? +

Yes! You select your preferred channel (🎮 Discord or 🎥 Jitsi video) when you book your appointment. That channel is locked for the session so both you and your provider are aligned.

Is my personal data encrypted and protected? +

Yes. All sessions, accounts, and clinical document dispatches are secured using Supabase Row-Level Security, multi-factor biometrics, and 256-bit encryption. Vault files stay strictly inside your local browser storage.

⚠️ Emergency & Crisis Notice Drozo Therapy is not an emergency psychiatric service. If you are experiencing suicidal thoughts, self-harm impulses, or a medical crisis, please call or text 988 or contact your local emergency number immediately.
